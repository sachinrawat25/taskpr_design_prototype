TASK PR — Seven Typography Directions

These are deliberately different categories, not variations on one idea. Each block leads with a large mixed-case specimen, because that is where a typeface shows its personality — uppercase with wide tracking hides it.

  • Judge the big line first — that is the typeface’s actual character.
  • Then the dark hero — that is how it appears on the real site.
  • Then the nav and eyebrow — small uppercase is where legibility breaks.
