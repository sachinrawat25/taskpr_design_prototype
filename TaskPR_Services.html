Questions

Frequently asked questions

What services does TASK PR offer?
Eight: public relations, influencer marketing, social media management, content creation and production, celebrity events and launches, celebrity relations and seeding, SEO, GEO and digital PR, and affiliate and media partnerships.
Which brands does TASK PR work with?
Fashion, accessories, footwear, beauty, lifestyle and interiors brands, from heritage names to growing direct-to-consumer labels.
Does TASK PR work in Germany as well as the UK?
Yes. TASK PR Ltd is based in London and TASK PR GmbH runs campaigns in Germany, so one team can plan and deliver across both markets.
What is GEO and how is it different from SEO?
SEO helps your website rank in search results. GEO (generative engine optimisation) helps your brand get mentioned and recommended in AI answers from tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. AI tools rely heavily on trusted editorial sources, so earned press coverage supports both.
Can we hire TASK PR for a single service?
Yes. You can start with one service, such as PR or influencer marketing, and add others as your brand grows. Every service is planned to work with the rest.
How does TASK PR measure results?
Monthly reports cover the measures that matter for each service: press coverage and reach, creator content and engagement, social growth, backlinks and search traffic, AI search mentions and affiliate sales.
